FAQ

Common questions answered

How long does a guest post placement take?
Typically 5–10 business days from brief approval to a live URL — content writing takes 3–5 days, then the host site's review and publish window takes 2–5 more. We'll give you an estimate specific to your chosen site when you confirm.
What DA/DR do your guest post sites have?
Our minimum quality floor is DA 20 with real organic traffic. Most placements fall in the DA 25–50 range. We share the exact DA, PA, DR, traffic estimate, and spam score for every site before you commit — you decide based on the real numbers.
Can I order a sample lead list before buying in bulk?
Yes — we offer a 25-contact sample for any niche so you can verify quality before committing to a larger list. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll discuss what's right for your target market.
Do you work with niches outside solar / electrical / electronics?
Our core depth is in those four technical verticals. For adjacent industries (HVAC, plumbing, construction, general B2B manufacturing) we can often source quality placements — reach out and we'll be honest about availability in your specific niche.
Is content writing included in guest post pricing?
Yes — when you buy a guest post placement through us, content writing is included at no extra charge. The $45/1,000 words rate applies when you need standalone articles or content for placements you're managing yourself.
